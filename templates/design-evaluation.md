## Reporting rule

State exactly which routes, states, browsers, viewports, participants, assistive technologies, and automated rules were covered. Automated accessibility checks detect only some problems. Screenshot similarity is not usability. A limited test is evidence for its scope, not universal proof. Avoid unqualified claims such as “fully accessible,” “WCAG-compliant,” “validated with users,” or “pixel-perfect.”
