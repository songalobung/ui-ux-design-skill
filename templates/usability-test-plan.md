## Neutral prompts

- “What are you looking for right now?”
- “What do you expect will happen if you choose that?”
- “What does this message mean to you?”
- “What would you do next?”
- “What, if anything, feels uncertain?”
- “Tell me what you think has happened so far.”

Avoid leading questions, teaching the interface, and treating stated preference as observed task success.
